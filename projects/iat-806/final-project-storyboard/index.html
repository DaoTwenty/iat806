Final Project Storyboard — Listening Loop

Draft

An instrument where audio is the prompt. A visitor feeds in sound, MIDI-GPT writes MIDI loops conditioned on it, and a radial sequencer with particle effects shows what the model heard and what it wrote.

Scope

  • Core (midpoint demo): frames 2–5. Audio in, MIDI-GPT, a loop playing on the ring, one control, basic particles.
  • Final: frames 6–8 and 10. Instant vs. next-loop controls, infilling, layering and feedback, exhibition mode.
  • Stretch: frame 9 extra inputs (sensors, OCR) and other audio sources such as Max/MSP, RAVE and Stable Audio.
