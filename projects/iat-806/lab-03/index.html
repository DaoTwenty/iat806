Lab 03 — Dancing Zombies

Pixel zombies that dance, groan, freeze and blow up.

How to play

Click the canvas first so the browser allows sound. Press Z to create a zombie (up to five at once) and click a zombie to make it groan. Press G to drop a grenade at a random spot, then drag it and let go: it always explodes, and it obliterates any zombie it lands on. Press space to freeze everything in ice and winter, and space again to thaw it. The background slowly cycles from night to day and back.

Sounds are CC0 from OpenGameArt (artisticdude, qubodup, EZduzziteh, NenadSimic, bart). Credits are in the sounds/ folders.
