Lab 02 — Repelling Moon

A moon that bounces around the canvas while you push it away with an Earth.

  • Sketch → Click and hold to repel the moon from your cursor. It grows and shrinks as it bounces off the edges.

Source code: github.com/DaoTwenty/IAT806-Lab02

Notes

While the mouse is held down, a repelling force pushes the moon directly away from the cursor, and the Earth (with a radial gradient glow) is drawn under it. The moon's speed is capped, and its size shrinks and grows between a minimum and maximum as it bounces.
