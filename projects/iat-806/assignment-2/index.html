Assignment 2 — Echo Drummer

Play the drums with your head.

How to play

Allow the camera and click Start. The game plays a short drum beat. Then it's your turn: play it back from memory by tilting your head LEFT for the kick and RIGHT for the snare, coming back to the middle between hits.

Score at least 50% to reach the next level; each level is longer and faster. Pass all 5 levels to win. Miss three times and it's game over. No camera? Use F for kick and J for snare.
