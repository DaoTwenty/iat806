Lab 05 — Fighting Pit

Notes

Fighters with random looks hunt each other down, trade punches and get knocked out flat until one is left standing. Press f to throw in a new fighter. Click or press a key to start the sound.

Music: "Crouch jumping in your walls" by Fupi. Punches: Independent.nu. Grunts: HaelDB. All CC0, from OpenGameArt.
