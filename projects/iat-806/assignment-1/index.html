Assignment 1 — The Ole Rag'n'Cat Pub

A fun mini-game that takes place in a rustic pub.

Instructions

Feel free to play with the piano yourself by pressing the keys.

You may also start or stop the ghost pianist by pressing 'p'. If you interrupt them more than 3 times, they might get angry...

Spawn ants by pressing 'a'. To get rid of them, try to squish them by clicking on them!

Finally, a surprise awaits you if you high five the secret cat...
